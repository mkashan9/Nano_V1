# LRN-05 manual test

Run the student app as a Senior learner.

1. Open **Profile** and tap **Your progress**.
   - The top card is **Up next** with a topic and a plain-language reason.
   - Under it, each subject shows "N of M done" in words, not just a bar.
2. Tap **Resume** on the recommendation. It should open that topic.
3. Go back to **Home** and tap the **Continue Learning** card. It should open the
   same topic the recommendation named.
4. Finish a topic, return to **Your progress**, and confirm the recommendation has
   moved on. The finished topic must not be suggested again.
5. Confirm a locked topic is never the recommendation, and that its subject still
   says how many topics are locked.
6. Switch to **Urdu**. The topic title, subject name, and the reason line should
   all read in Urdu.
7. Pull down to refresh. The screen should reload without flashing an error.

Reply `NEXT` to approve, or `FIX: …` if a suggestion looks wrong.
