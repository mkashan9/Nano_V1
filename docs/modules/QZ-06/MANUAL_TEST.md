# QZ-06 manual test

Run `student_app` as Junior.

1. Open Counting → **Take quiz**. Before answering, confirm nothing on screen
   names a correct answer or shows a **Why:** line.
2. Answer wrongly on purpose and finish. Confirm the results screen shows
   **Score: …%**, "0 of 1 correct", the pass mark, **Your answer: …**,
   **Correct answer: …**, and a **Why: …** explanation.
3. Tap **Retake quiz**. Confirm a fresh question 1 opens with no answer
   pre-selected, then answer correctly and finish. The review should now show
   your answer with no correction line.
4. Switch to Senior and finish a quiz via **Review & finish**. Confirm the same
   score, review, and explanations appear under **Your results**.
5. Optional: on the progress screen, confirm a topic whose quiz you failed is
   still suggested with the "Worth another look" reason.

Reply `NEXT` to approve, or `FIX: …` if the review, retake, or explanations look
wrong.
