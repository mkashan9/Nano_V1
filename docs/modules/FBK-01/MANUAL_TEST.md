# FBK-01 manual test

Use a **teacher** account with an active assignment and roster students.

1. Open **Feedback** in the teacher shell.
2. Pick a class/subject and a student.
3. Choose category **Effort**, enter a note, tap **Save draft**.
4. Confirm the note appears as `draft`; tap **Edit**, enable **Publish now**, save.
5. Confirm status becomes `published` and **Edit** is gone.
