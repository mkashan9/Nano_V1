# STU-06 manual test

1. Open student Home (junior or senior).
2. Tap the notifications bell.
3. Confirm seeded items appear (e.g. "New topic unlocked", "Assembly note").
4. Tap **Unread** — read items (Assembly note) disappear.
5. Tap **All**, then tap an unread item.
6. Confirm a snackbar shows `Link: …` and the item no longer appears under **Unread**.
