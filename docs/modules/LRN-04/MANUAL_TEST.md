# LRN-04 manual test

Sign in as a Senior learner (the long fixture is senior Science content).

1. Open **Science → Ecosystems in depth** and press **Start**. The player opens
   at 0:00 and the chapter name reads *What an ecosystem is*.
2. Drag the scrubber to about **10:50** and press play. Within seconds the
   *Quick break* prompt appears, playback pauses, and the chapter name has
   changed to *Food webs*.
3. Press **Keep watching**. Playback resumes and the prompt does not return,
   even if you drag back across 11:00.
4. Turn on **Classroom Mode** in profile settings, reopen the topic, drag to
   about **29:50**, and play. The optional *Quick break* at 30:00 no longer
   interrupts.
5. Still in Classroom Mode, drag to about **21:50** and play. The required
   *Think back* prompt does appear, and it says progress is paused until you
   answer. Watch the **Watched** figure: it stops climbing at 22:00.
6. Press **Keep watching**, play on, and confirm **Watched** starts climbing
   again.
7. Open **Math → Adding small numbers** (no-skip-ahead content). The player says
   you cannot skip ahead, and dragging the scrubber to the end leaves the head a
   little past what you have watched rather than at the end.

What should not happen: a prompt inside the *Check yourself* segment
(19:00–22:00), the same prompt twice, or a required prompt that can be dismissed
without answering.
