# LRN-03 Manual Test

1. Open the student app as a Junior learner, open Math, tap **Counting to 20**,
   then press **Start**. The player opens on the topic.
2. Press play and watch for about twenty seconds. **Watched** climbs and the
   line under it counts down the seconds still owed.
3. Drag the position slider to the end. The clock reads `2:00 / 2:00`, but
   **Mark complete** stays disabled and seconds are still owed — skipping earns
   nothing.
4. Turn **Captions** off and on. With captions on, the caption line follows the
   position; switch the app to Urdu and the Urdu caption appears.
5. Leave it playing until the owed seconds reach zero (about two minutes of
   real playback), then press **Mark complete**. The page shows **Completed**
   and the button is disabled.
6. Go back to Math. **Counting to 20** reads as completed and **Adding small
   numbers** is now unlocked.
7. Open a topic you left partway, press **Start**, and confirm the player opens
   at the second you stopped rather than at zero.
